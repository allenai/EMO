Intermediate report: loading run state.

The exhaustive DCLM document universe is assigned by a SHA-256 ordering over seed, canonical shard path, and zero-based document index. Validation, test, and training are mutually exclusive; their union is the complete source pool.

The repeated 1B and unique 5B samples are the same independent uniform document samples used by dense Step 2. No dense checkpoint is reused.

Adaptive LR ladder: epoch 1 tests 5e-4 and 1e-3, starting from the dense optimum. Through epoch 5, each later epoch includes the previous MoE optimum, that epoch's dense optimum, and one higher rung, with every LR advancing only through its own matching pre-decay checkpoint chain. From epoch 6 onward, only 1e-3 runs for unique and repeated data. Epochs 8 and 10 are executed as two sequential phases inside one Beaker allocation per regime, retaining separate evaluations and checkpoint chains without a second scheduling wait.

Statistical significance: every LR and unique-vs-repeated conclusion reports the paired difference, a two-sided 95% confidence interval, and yes/no significance. Validation-loss intervals use a paired document-level bootstrap over the same 79,690 documents (100,000,118 real tokens); accuracy intervals use paired downstream examples. A result is significant only when its interval excludes zero.
